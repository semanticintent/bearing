# Catalog domain glossary

## Copy, title, edition

A **copy** is one physical item with a barcode. A **title** is the
bibliographic work shared across many copies. An **edition** is a specific
printing of a title, identified by ISBN — copies belong to one edition and
editions are not interchangeable with titles.

These three are not interchangeable and cataloguers will correct you.

## Availability

"Available" means status `AV` and not withdrawn. It does not mean "not
checked out" — a copy in processing or mending is unavailable but also not
checked out.

Branch managers report on availability weekly. Circulation staff watch it
daily. When they say "the availability number" they mean different things:
managers mean the month-to-date average, circulation staff mean right now.

## Who uses this application

Circulation staff, daily, for shelf location and availability. Branch
managers, weekly, for reporting. Finance, at year-end, for collection
valuation and deaccession accounting.

Finance is the only group that cares about `AcquiredOn` and `WithdrawnOn`.
